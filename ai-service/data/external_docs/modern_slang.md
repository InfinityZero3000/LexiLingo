# Modern English Slang & Trends 2026

### Rizz (Noun/Verb)
"Rizz" is a shortened form of "charisma". It refers to someone's ability to attract or charm others, especially in a romantic context.
Example: "He has so much rizz, he can talk to anyone."
Note: High rizz means very charming.

### Delulu (Adjective)
Short for "delusional". It is often used in a humorous way to describe someone who holds unrealistic beliefs or over-idealistic fantasies about a situation.
Example: "Thinking I will win the lottery without buying a ticket is pure delulu."
Note: "Stay delulu" is a common phrase meaning to stay optimistic against the odds.

### Understood the Assignment (Phrase)
Used when someone does something perfectly or exceeds expectations.
Example: "She wore that vintage dress to the 20s party; she clearly understood the assignment."

### Tea (Noun)
Slang for gossip or juicy information.
Example: "What happened at the party? Spill the tea!"
Note: "Spilling the tea" means sharing gossip.

### Rent Free (Phrase)
When you can't stop thinking about something or someone, they are living "rent free" in your head.
Example: "That catchy song is living rent free in my head all day."
