## 💡 Why XP Badges Were Removed

XP badges (xp-hunter, xp-warrior, xp-champion, xp-legend) were removed because:
- **Redundancy**: Level milestone badges (lv25, lv50, lv100, lv150, lv200, lv300, lv500) already track player progression
- **Simplification**: Reduces badge clutter and focuses on meaningful achievements
- **Better UX**: Players focus on level milestones which are clearer progression indicators
- **Duplication**: XP thresholds (100, 500, 1k, 5k) overlap poorly with level system
